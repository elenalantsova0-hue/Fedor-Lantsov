Black Woman Portrait

Original artwork

Artwork information

Oil on linen · 50 × 70 cm · 2021

Black Woman Portrait combines intimate portraiture with a bold symbolic foreground. The sitter is shown close to the picture plane, her braided hair and serene, slightly averted gaze rendered with smooth tonal transitions. Warm geometric shapes in gold, amber, terracotta, and deep brown create an architectural rhythm behind her, while a black panther occupies the lower foreground and adds visual weight to the composition. Soft, even illumination describes the face without harsh contrast, allowing subtle expression and carefully observed features to remain central. The relationship between portrait and animal gives the painting a distinctive presence while leaving its meaning open to personal interpretation. Executed in oil on linen, the work brings stylized realism into conversation with classical fine art portraiture. It is a compelling choice for collectors seeking an original oil painting, contemporary portrait art, or richly colored figurative artwork. The concentrated 50 × 70 cm format supports both close viewing and a strong decorative impact in a curated interior.
