Female Figure

Original artwork

Artwork information

Oil on linen · 50 × 70 cm · 2021

Female Figure is a focused study of expression, light, and turning form. A woman with voluminous wavy hair looks upward and to the side, introducing a sense of anticipation beyond the frame. The bust-length composition is set against a dark, indistinct background, allowing dramatic side light to shape the face, shoulder, and movement of the hair. Warm browns, tan, and cream establish a restrained palette associated with Baroque-inspired painting, while a heavy piece of fabric crosses one shoulder and adds weight to the silhouette. Painted in oil on linen, the work combines classical realism with the directness of a studio figure study. Smooth transitions are balanced by more visible texture in the hair and drapery, keeping the surface active at close range. This original figurative oil painting is suited to collectors seeking classical figure art, expressive portrait painting, and contemporary realism. Its 50 × 70 cm vertical format makes it intimate enough for close viewing yet visually strong within a larger collection.
