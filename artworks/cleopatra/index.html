Cleopatra

Original artwork

Artwork information

Oil on linen · 70 × 60 cm · 2021

Cleopatra reimagines the Egyptian queen through a warm, theatrical interior filled with carved reliefs and golden light. The dark-haired figure leans forward, extending open hands toward the viewer in a gesture that feels both inviting and ceremonial. Large hoop earrings, an ornate gold necklace, and a turquoise centerpiece bring concentrated color and texture to the portrait. Light white fabric softens the transition between the figure and the ochre, terracotta, and deep brown setting. Directional illumination highlights the face, jewelry, and hands, while the surrounding reliefs recede into atmospheric shadow. Painted in oil on linen, the work combines mythology art, historical portraiture, and classical realism without presenting the scene as an archaeological reconstruction. Cleopatra is designed for collectors who value original figurative oil painting, Egyptian-inspired fine art, and richly detailed female portraiture. Its horizontal 70 × 60 cm format encourages a broad visual dialogue between the central figure, her expressive gesture, and the layered architecture behind her.
