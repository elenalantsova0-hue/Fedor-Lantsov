Shaman Dance

Original artwork

Artwork information

Oil on linen · 80 × 50 cm · 2019

Shaman Dance centers a female figure in a symmetrical, ritual-like gesture, with both arms raised and palms directed outward. Her eyes are closed, and large curved horns rise from the head, establishing a powerful silhouette against a glowing field of orange, yellow, and red. Cooler dark tones across the horns and patterned fabric at the shoulders sharpen the contrast with the radiant background. The horizontal format gives the raised arms space to expand, while the frontal pose creates stillness within the suggestion of dance and energy. Painted in oil on linen, the work is more expressive and textured than a conventional classical portrait, using visible brushwork to reinforce movement and atmosphere. Shaman Dance will interest collectors seeking spiritual art, symbolic figurative painting, contemporary mythology, and original oil artwork with a commanding focal presence. At 80 × 50 cm, it is designed to read clearly across a room while offering layered color and surface detail at closer distance.
