Aurora

Original artwork

Artwork information

Oil on linen · 50 × 70 cm · 2019

Aurora is a vertical figurative oil painting built around stillness, profile, and the expressive power of chiaroscuro. A woman with long light-brown hair turns away from the viewer, her crossed arm creating a protective gesture across the composition. Deep red drapery gathers around the lower form, introducing a rich color accent against a dark, studio-like background. Strong directional light travels across the shoulder, hair, and side of the face, while surrounding browns and ochres dissolve gradually into shadow. The restrained setting keeps attention on posture and tonal structure rather than narrative detail. Painted in oil on linen, the work reflects the discipline of classical realism while retaining a contemporary emotional distance. Aurora will appeal to collectors searching for an original figurative oil painting, a refined female portrait, or atmospheric fine art shaped by Old Master lighting. Its warm palette and elegant silhouette allow it to sit comfortably within traditional interiors, modern spaces, and collections centered on portrait painting or contemporary realism.
