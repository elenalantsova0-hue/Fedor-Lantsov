Fedor Lantsov in the atelier

Artist statement

Painting Light,
Form & Memory

Rooted in the classical European atelier method, the practice balances rigorous traditional technique with psychological narrative. Each painting is built through drawing, tonal structure, finely ground pigments and translucent glazes.

“The classical tradition is not a destination. It is a language through which the present can be seen more clearly.”

The studio focuses on mythology, portraiture and the human figure, treating every canvas as an extended study of presence and time.
